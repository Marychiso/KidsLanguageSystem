🐾 Luna’s Animal Park Adventure

Luna visits a small animal park. The sun is shining and everything feels calm and she sees friendly animals waiting to say hello.

“The animals begin to move and play together. The dog runs happily, the cat jumps around, and the bird flies in the sky. Luna laughs as she watches them.”

Soon it is time to go home. Luna waves and says, ‘Goodbye, my animal friends!’ The animals gather close as if waving back.”
