Ally’s Alphabet Walk

Ally walks through a bright garden. She sees many letters around her.

A - Apple

B - Ball

C - Cat

D - Dog

E - Elephant

She keeps walking and finds more letters along the way.

J - Juice

K - Kite

L - Lion

M - Monkey

At the end, all the letters from A to Z stood and shined together. Ally smiled and said, "So many letters!"

She waved goodbye and walked home smiling ✨
